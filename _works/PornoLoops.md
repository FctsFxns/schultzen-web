PornoLoops is a video-based work investigating the repetitive structures of desire embedded in pornographic imagery and audiovisual consumption.

Using looping sequences extracted and transformed through montage and repetition, the project approaches pornography less as explicit representation than as a visual system organised through rhythm, projection, anticipation, and endless circulation.

By isolating and repeating fragments of movement and gesture, the work exposes how desire becomes constructed through mechanical reproduction and temporal repetition.

Developed during the artist’s transition from analog filmmaking towards live audiovisual and installation practices, the project anticipates later investigations into mediated sexuality, emotional projection, and collective imaginaries.

**Forms**

Looped video installation: repeating moving-image sequences exploring the rhythm and temporality of pornographic representation

Audiovisual montage: fragmented visual compositions generated through repetition and temporal manipulation

These forms operate through accumulation and recurrence, transforming explicit imagery into rhythmic and affective visual structures.

**Context**

The project forms part of an early body of work investigating sexuality, mediated desire, and audiovisual repetition through collage and moving-image experimentation.

It establishes thematic concerns that later reappear in works such as Trash Food Porn, Perra Perdida, and Oráculos de los Márgenes.
